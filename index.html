The grain journey

Follow the grain, from field to payment.

One load moves through the farm. Its records stay connected.

  1. 01 / Harvest

    Harvest starts the record.

    From the field, grain heads to on-farm storage or straight to the elevator.

  2. 02 / Storage

    Know what’s in the bin.

    Keep estimated inventory by bin and see what your farm has in storage.

  3. 03 / Delivery

    Capture the load that moved.

    Scan the delivery ticket, review the details, and save it to your farm’s records.

  4. 04 / Connected records

    See what was committed, delivered, and paid.

Our story

Built around real grain operations.

BinFlow started on our family's grain farm in southern Indiana. I saw how much time went into collecting tickets, updating spreadsheets, and keeping track of grain moving between bins, contracts, and buyers.

I wanted to build something that made those everyday jobs easier—not another complicated system farmers have to learn.

Eli Arnold, Founder of BinFlow
