Tickets, bins, contracts, and payments stay connected through the same real-world workflow.

One workflow instead of five loose records

Keep grain tickets, inventory, and sales records together.

BinFlow focuses on the operational details farms actually need: where grain came from, where it went, which contract it belongs to, and whether payment has been received.

01

Scan and review tickets

Take or upload a ticket photo. Review the extracted fields, correct anything that needs attention, and save the load.

02

Keep bins current

Track estimated bushels and capacity by bin. Saved tickets and manual grain adjustments keep your records current.

03

Track contract balances

Assign delivered bushels to spot sales or contracts and track the bushels remaining on each contract.

04

Control farm access

Admins manage farm records and invite team members. Employees can scan tickets into their farm with access suited to their role.

Simple enough for the field, useful enough for the office

From paper ticket to clean farm record.

BinFlow is designed and tested around the way grain actually moves on working farms. Employees can capture the ticket, admins can review and reconcile, and the farm keeps one shared source of truth.

1

Scan the ticket

Capture commodity, bushels, destination, hauler, moisture, and other ticket details.

2

Assign the sale

Mark the load as spot or choose the contract it should count against.

3

Update inventory

Subtract from the selected bin and keep crop totals current.

4

Reconcile payment

Record checks or other payments received, including partial payments. See which delivered tickets still need payment. BinFlow tracks these records; it does not move money.
