# BinFlow dashboard and contract naming

Reviewed November 11, 2026, against the production source at `9351b2c`. The screens use the fictional Prairie Ridge Farms demo. The calculation audit traces the application and schema in source. It does not certify individual farms' recorded balances or payment data.

1. **Dashboard entry.** The farm name and Scan, Bins, History, and Contracts actions are clear. The desktop screen repeats inventory quantities across the KPI row, crop chart, and bin cards. The phone screen puts six tiles ahead of the work that needs attention. The sticky navigation also covers part of the viewport while scrolling. Screenshot inspection alone does not establish keyboard, contrast, or screen-reader compliance.
2. **Position and payments.** The existing screen groups contracts and payments together, but a count of contracts with balances does not say how many bushels are owed. The unpaid quantity looks like a physical or financial balance even though the whole delivery is counted when it is partly paid. The redesign makes commodity balances, open deliveries, and payment follow-up distinct.
3. **Activity.** The old weekly section contains useful ticket and adjustment details, but a long list on the dashboard competes with open work on mobile. The redesign shows the six latest recorded entries in the last seven days. Full history and inventory remain reachable through the existing navigation.

## Current metrics

Unless noted, fields come from `GET /api/dashboard` in `server/index.js`, `server/supabaseStore.js`, and `server/weeklySummary.js`. The old API's farm-filtered snapshots have no explicit pagination. A configured Data API row cap can make totals and activity incomplete for large farms. The old code is preserved for existing clients.

| Display | Calculation and inputs | Operational value and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corn inventory and soybean inventory | `kpis.total_corn_inventory` and `total_bean_inventory` sum public `bins.current_bushels` for exact `crop_type` values Corn and Beans. `publicBin` clamps the stored value to the estimated capacity. | Useful recorded inventory. Opening quantities and manual corrections may be estimates. Other crop spellings are missed by the two exact filters. |
| Total bushels stored | `kpis.total_bushels_stored` sums the public balance for every returned bin. | Useful as a storage total. Combining crops obscures what can fulfill any one obligation. |
| Total tickets scanned | `kpis.total_tickets_scanned` is the length of the returned `tickets` snapshot, including manual and older records. | Neither a true scan count nor a useful daily priority. Deleted tickets disappear and a row cap can lower the count. |
| Total bushels sold | `kpis.total_bushels_sold` sums `inventory_transactions.bushel_amount` where `transaction_type = TICKET_SALE`. | Records ticket-linked inventory activity. It does not capture field deliveries with no bin transaction or demonstrate pricing or payment. A requested removal may differ from the applied amount. |
| Number of active bins | `kpis.active_bins` counts all returned bin rows. | Indicates configured bins. There is no active-status filter here, and empty bins count. |
| Unpaid delivered | `finance.unpaid_delivered_bushels` sums `tickets.bushels` where `payment_status !== 'Paid'`. | The whole quantity of partly paid tickets is included. Payment dollars do not establish a reliably unpaid bushel quantity. |
| Unpaid estimated | `finance.unpaid_estimated_dollars` sums `max(revenue - amount_received, 0)` for those same tickets. `revenue` is computed from recorded ticket price × bushels. | Useful as a rough follow-up estimate. Missing price can look like zero due. Status and received amount depend on entry and are not verified against settlement records. |
| Open contract balances | `finance.contracts_with_remaining_bushels` counts non-archived contracts whose calculated remainder exceeds zero and whose status is not exactly `Closed`. | Counts contracts, not bushels. Filled or cancelled statuses and spelling variants can distort the label. |
| Unassigned tickets | `finance.unassigned_tickets` counts returned tickets with `assignment_status = 'Unassigned'`. | Useful cleanup work. It depends on the stored status and says nothing about price or delivery. |
| Inventory by crop chart | `charts.inventory_by_crop` repeats the Corn and Beans KPI sums. The bar scale uses the largest displayed crop balance. | Compares crop totals. A full bar means the largest crop, not full storage. |
| Storage utilization | `charts.storage_utilization` divides total public bin balances by sum of `bins.estimated_capacity_bushels`; null when capacity is zero. | Useful for handling capacity planning. The denominator is estimated and an aggregate hides individual full bins. |
| Bin overview | `bin_overview` includes name, crop, current balance, estimated capacity and rounded percentage from `publicBin`. | Useful operational detail already available under Bins. The percentage inherits both capacity and balance uncertainty. |
| Weekly reporting window and email note | `weekly_summary.period_label` spans the latest Sunday at noon Eastern to the dashboard snapshot time. The UI advertises a Sunday email schedule. | Explains the window. It does not confirm an email was sent or received. |
| Weekly ticket movement and entries | `ticket_bushels` and `ticket_count` sum/count `tickets` whose `created_at` falls in the window. | Shows recorded deliveries and their entries. A backdated delivery belongs to its entry week here, rather than its delivery date. |
| Weekly manually added and removed | `grain_added_bushels` and `grain_removed_bushels` split manual deltas by sign. Add uses `bushel_amount`; remove uses negative `applied_bushel_amount ?? bushel_amount`; manual adjustment uses new minus previous balance. | Helpful activity signal. A capped addition can overstate recorded movement; opening estimates and estimate corrections are excluded from these legacy totals. |
| Weekly manual net and adjustments count | `manual_net_bushels` is added minus removed. `manual_adjustment_count` counts eligible manual transactions within the window. | Net can hide offsetting changes. This figure excludes ticket sales and does not explain all inventory changes. |
| Weekly crop totals and activity rows | `by_crop` groups weekly ticket bushels and manual deltas by crop. Ticket entries display number, crop, source, destination, quantity, and `created_at`. Manual entries show transaction type, bin, crop, before/after and time. | Useful audit trail. The record time and delivery date mean different things. Current UI may list every returned activity row. |

`payments_received_this_month` and `charts.recent_ticket_activity` are emitted by the old API but are not shown on this dashboard. The former sums `amount_received` for ticket `payment_date` in the server's current month. It is not a cash journal. The latter returns the first seven tickets from a snapshot sorted by creation time, projecting ticket date, crop, and quantity.

## Proposed metrics and limits

- **In bins** is the sum of recorded `bins.current_bushels` by normalized commodity. Corn is grouped case-insensitively and Beans/Soybeans are one crop. A bin balance remains an estimate when the underlying entry is an estimate.
- **Still to deliver** is the sum, per crop, of `max(0, contracted_bushels - applied)` for contracts marked Open. Applied is the sum of current CONTRACT assignment quantities tied to that contract's internal UUID. Multiple allocations on one ticket are summed. Spot allocations are excluded. Each remainder is floored before aggregation. **Applied to open contracts** shows those same applied bushels against the total contracted quantity on Open contracts. This does not count grain on closed or archived contracts.
- **After open obligations / recorded shortfall** is recorded bin bushels minus the open remainder for the same commodity. It is a planning comparison. The app does not know grade, location restrictions, third-party commitments, future harvest, or which stored grain is priced or reserved. Negative coverage does not imply a missed deadline. Positive coverage does not imply unpriced or marketable grain.
- **Open deliveries** shows up to five contracts by remaining quantity. The delivery window is the saved free text verbatim, with an empty state for missing text. Reliable due-soon or overdue flags require a structured date or date range and a product rule about deadlines. Status Open is explicit; whether partially fulfilled records marked Filled, Closed, or another status should be surfaced as discrepancies is a product decision.
- **Payments to review** shows **Tickets to review**, the count of existing farm tickets explicitly marked `Not paid` or `Partially paid` across all crop years. Paid and deleted tickets do not count. **Open History** selects the combined **Needs payment review** filter and includes archived crop years so the initial results match the dashboard. The redesigned dashboard no longer requests payment amounts or calculates a dollar figure. The audit found that the earlier estimate excluded blank-price contract deliveries, used a single ticket price for mixed allocations, and treated blank partial-payment amounts as zero. Contract price alone also cannot establish a buyer's net settlement. The count describes review work based on recorded status. Existing payment records, edits, and the legacy dashboard API are preserved.
- **Recent grain activity** uses the six latest eligible rows by `created_at` within the prior seven days. Delivery entries show ticket bushels. Bin additions, removals, adjustments, and estimate entries show the recorded `new_bin_balance - previous_bin_balance`. Ticket-sale transactions are omitted to avoid listing the same delivery twice. This is recent entry activity, not a net inventory ledger.

The new dashboard requests a separate response variant via `GET /api/dashboard?grain_position=true`. It reads farm-scoped rows with paging to avoid the old fixed snapshot cap. REST reads are separate statements, so a write during a refresh can produce a temporary mismatch. Exercise large farms and concurrent changes in disposable staging before release. Where data exists, potentially useful later metrics include unassigned tickets by age, bin headroom, contract fulfillment by crop year after adding a reliable contract year, and payment aging after settlement due dates and receipts are modeled.

## Naming and rollout

`contracts.id` remains the UUID used by ticket allocations, balances, and inventory commands. `contracts.contract_id` stays the official number with its existing farm uniqueness rule. The proposed nullable `contracts.display_name` is an optional 100-character alias. Duplicated aliases are allowed; the official number and buyer disambiguate them. An old client omitting the field preserves a saved name. A deliberate blank in the new form clears it. Contract cards and dashboard rows put the name first. Scanner, split, and ticket-edit native pickers put it first in the option label and show the chosen alias in bold next to its identifying details. Native dropdowns render each option as plain text. Contracts, pickers, and opted-in Ticket History search by both identifiers. History resolves aliases from current contract records, so a rename changes the label shown for a past ticket without rewriting that ticket. Existing CSV columns and weekly email format are unchanged. Outside the combined payment review filter, History search still follows the legacy history snapshot's row and archive filters.
